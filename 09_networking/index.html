Week 9: Networking

This week, Ethan, Emma, Arden and myself decided to make horrible texting devices. Our plan was to create a horrible, portable system with three features: a speaker, to loudly and mandatorally broadcast messages; a potentiometer, to make sending messages as inefficient as possible; and a button, to send texts and confirm letter choices. We started by making the pot keyboard, which took an embarrasingly long time to debug. That was a lot of fun to do -- Emma and Arden handled most of the wiring on that, while Ethan and I fought C++. I then spent a while trying to do the TTS, which, after battling github libraries, Spanish, and Wifi (the initial plan was to use wifi, don't ask, I'm still traumatized by that hour and a half of failure), we had to cut our losses and admit defeat -- we're planning on completing it later. Luckily, we'd already managed to make the xiaos talk to each other, and we can send messages (typed very ineffeciently on our horrible hardware) to Serials. We do want to finish this set of bad-phones for the bit, so this will be completed at some point soon!

The first step was the keyboard. We quickly wired up a breadboard with a button, speaker, and pot, and then wrote up some code.
