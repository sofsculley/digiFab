Week 4: Programming

I decided to make a little dragon that would come to life if you tried to steal his treasure. I was inspired by one of the examples, which used a piezo to open a door when knocked on. To show that he was paying attention, the dragon would move its wings and its eyes would light up.

I started with playing around with the wing mechanism. I settled on a bit of a ridiculous method, using strings on screws (attached to a motor) to pull down the wings, which would then be released, and snap back together with a spring. (I then went on a detour to try and do the exact physics of it, before my friend Charles pointed out that I was just procrastinating and did not need to know the exact torque needed. Here's me having fun finding the spring constant anyways).

Once I was satisfied that this could theoretically work, I moved on to programming the microcontroller. I decided to start by making sure that I could do it with a button, and spent a while trying to figure out how to run multiple circuts simulatenously.
