Week 8: CNC

Emma and I are in the throes of making a massive gummy bear!! His name is Idiot. Look at him -- isn't it fitting??

Our orignial plan was to CNC him on Sunday, and then make a silicone mold to cast in gelatin. We hit two snags along the way -- one, the printer was down on Sunday, which obviously made that impossible; and two, the massive gummy bear had to be shrunk from the dreamed foot and a half to the realistic 3 inches tall.
We did actually hit another, more major snag -- we kept failing to think in negatives. We had to redesign the mold at least twice, and forgot that the walls should be negative both times. At the very least, I learned how to do that, because I refuse face that horror again. Here's us crashing out after realizing the mold didn't have walls:

Unfortunately, I'm sorry to say that we didn't finish Idiot in time :( We're currently setting another mold overnight, after Emma reshaped our mold to (hopefully) allow for actual walls on the silicone. Wish us luck! Hopefully Idiot will be in the flesh by tomorrow night.
