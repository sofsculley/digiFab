Week 3: Fabrication

This week, Emma, Arden, and I worked together to make a seascape food chain (we felt really smug when we realized that Sofia Emma Arden spelt sea). None of us felt confident in ourselves before working on this, and I'm really glad we worked together, and made a fun thing.

Our first step was to come up with concepts. We settled on fish pretty quickly, and Emma drew some beautiful little guys on Sketchbook, which we then vectorized and threw into Fusion. (I love the base sketches, by the way. I'm going to try and turn them into earrings.)

Rather foolishly, we decided that the best course was to engrave them on cardboard. It didn't really work, but it did take us half and hour at the laser cutter, and so for everyone's benifit, we decided they were good enough.

We also designed a box to house our motor, and came up with a way to mount the motor. Unfortunately, our motor spun th wrong way, and so we spent a while workshopping how to fix the mount today to allow for it being turned upsidedown. Good thing no one has to see the inside of the box....

After we made it, we quickly realized that our thing spun far too fast. We tried to trial-and-error our way to a solution, and then tried to do physics, but, at the end of the day, nothing worked. (Something looked like it worked, and then never worked again, so.... yeah)

Anyways, here's the final result!
